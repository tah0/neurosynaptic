PHYSICS NOTES
-------------
The ALD1105PBL operates in the subthreshold (weak-inversion) regime:

  I_D = I_0 * exp(κ · V_GS / V_t)

where κ ≈ 0.5–0.7 (subthreshold slope factor, body effect), V_t = kT/q ≈ 26mV.

BJTs in forward-active operate as:

  I_C = I_S * exp(V_BE / V_t)

with κ = 1.0 exactly (no body effect).

Consequences:
  - κ shift from ~0.6 → 1.0 changes the effective transconductance gain
    by a factor of ~1.67. Bias voltages must be recalibrated for fabrication.
  - BJTs require nonzero base current (~I_C / β, β ≈ 100–200). The existing
    bias chain through VR1/R? must source this base current for Q2.
  - Q1 (PNP mirror) emitters are tied to +5V; no external base connection
    needed (C2 is diode-connected internally by the CurrentMirror symbol).
  - Factory-matched ALD transistors are replaced by discrete parts.
    Hand-match Q1's transistor pair if mirror accuracy is critical.
  - BJT subthreshold (I_C < ~1 µA) is poorly characterized and mismatched;
    the circuit is reliable above ~10 µA quiescent current.

For bench/topology validation, BJT substitution is acceptable.
For neuromorphic fabrication targets requiring precise κ, restore ALD1105PBL
or use a laser-trimmed matched BJT pair (e.g., MAT02, BCM61).
